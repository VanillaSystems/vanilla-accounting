# Mission

People who run a small company should spend their time on their business, not on their books.
Vanilla Accounting takes the bookkeeping off them: it automates as much of it as it can, and it
lets every person who uses it automate the rest themselves, by asking for it in their own words.

## How the work is shared

Every task in the books goes to the cheapest worker that does it right:

1. **Code first.** What a rule can decide is decided by code: reading a bank statement, the
   figures a receipt states plainly, a duplicate, a payment that matches a receipt by amount
   and number, a VAT return from the bookings. Code is fast, free, the same every time, and
   works without a subscription.
2. **The AI for judgement.** What needs judgement goes to the AI: reading a crumpled photo,
   which account a purchase belongs to, whether a lunch was private, what a person means by
   «mach das richtig». The AI takes as much work off the person as it safely can.
3. **The person for decisions only they can make.** A person is asked only what neither code
   nor the AI can know: whether a payment was private, whether a sale carried VAT, whether to
   file. Each question is asked once, in plain words, with what the app would do.

## Every correction teaches

When a person corrects what the app did, by hand or by a wish, the app keeps the correction and
learns from it: the next receipt of that kind is booked the right way without asking again. A
correction is never silently overwritten by the next run.

## People extend the automation by talking to it

The AI sidebar is how a person changes how the app works, not only what is in the books: «buch
Swisscom immer auf Telefon», «schick jeder Rechnung den Rapport als PDF mit», «lies jeden Montag
das Postfach», «frag mich bei Barbezügen über 500 Franken». The AI turns such a wish into a rule,
a workflow step or a setting, shows it plainly, and the person keeps it with one click. No code,
no files, no git: the person never needs to know how it is built.

## Always in the person's hands

- **Explainable.** Every automatic booking says why: which receipt, which rule, which run.
- **Reversible.** Every change, by the app, the AI or a person, can be taken back.
- **Possible without the AI.** Everything the AI does, a person can also do by hand: an
  accountant without a subscription, an auditor with a copy of the books, an owner whose
  subscription lapsed. The AI makes the work faster; it is never the only way.
- **Safe.** A filed period never changes. Corrections are booked in the open period.

## Who it is for

The owner who does the office on the side, the accountant who keeps the books of many small
firms, the auditor who checks them. Each of them should find that most of the work is done
already, that the rest is a question they can answer, and that whatever they still do by hand,
they can hand over to the app by saying so.

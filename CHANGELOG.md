# Changelog

What changed in each version of Vanilla Accounting. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and the version numbers follow
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/) as [`docs/releasing.md`](docs/releasing.md) applies
it to this project. `npm run update` prints the sections between your old version and the new one.

Two markers flag an entry you must read before you update:

- **⚠ figures**: the change alters a computed figure (a VAT Ziffer, a balance, a return) of an
  open period. A filed quarter's figures never change. After the update, `npm run test:books`
  shows the difference; check it, then accept it with `npm run test:books:update`.
- **⚠ action**: you must do something by hand, as the entry says.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- The AI reads the VAT facts of a payment, the app computes the VAT code (issue #64). For each booking line the AI now says what the receipt shows: Swiss VAT charged or not, the rate, Bezugsteuer, or that the amount is the VAT itself. It writes no code. The app takes the letter from the account (M on material and goods, I on operating costs and investments, V on revenue), the minus from the direction of the money, and `-1`/`-2` from the kind of booking. An AI answer that still contains a code keeps only its rate, so I81 on a 4xxx line is booked M81. A rule can name a rate, as in `vat: 81` or `vat: 8.1`, and the account decides the code. A rule that names a code, as in `vat: I81`, still works and gets the letter of its account. Entries that people booked and closed quarters do not change.
- **⚠ figures** The booking applies the app's own VAT knowledge in code, not only as text for the AI (issue #45). On a line the AI booked, the input-tax code now follows the receipt rules of `processes/booking.guide.yaml#purchase_vat`: a rate code with no receipt, or with receipts that show no Swiss VAT, becomes the zero code of its account (I0, M0); a zero code beside a receipt that shows one Swiss rate on the line's whole amount becomes that rate's code (I81, M81). Where a receipt taxes only part of a payment (a customs invoice, a mixed cart) or the AI split the payment into several lines with codes, the AI's codes stand. A public business file can state a fact the app carries out, as `apply:` on a rule of its `booking.guide.yaml#purchase_vat`: Shopify's payouts claim no input tax on their fees (the monthly bill does, `pv-shopify-fees`). Whether a receipt that could not be read counts as Swiss VAT is the company's choice, not a fact of the business: `apply: { unreadReceipt: 8.1 }` goes only in the company's own file of the business (`knowledge/suppliers/<id>.yaml`), and without it an unread receipt decides nothing. The shipped Coop Pronto file states facts only, and neither it, the AI's instructions nor AGENTS.md assume 8.1 % for an unreadable Coop Pronto receipt any more. Such a booking says which rule decided and is marked as the app's check («die Vorsteuer folgt dem Beleg …»). The company's word goes first: a payment one of its rules decides never reaches this, and a business whose file in `knowledge/` has purchase_vat rules of its own keeps the AI's codes. A firm on the net tax rate method or not registered books its purchases without a code as before. `npm run check-processes` checks every `apply:`. Filed quarters are untouched: only new bookings of open periods, so the next booking run can move a payment's input tax between I81 and I0 (Ziffer 400/405); the demo goldens are unchanged.
- Two new areas in the dock, Geschäftspartner (G P) and Wissen (G E) (docs/0.4.0/knowledge.md). Geschäftspartner lists the businesses the company works with: its own partner files, the public businesses its payments route to, and the customers of Rechnungen (one partner, two views: a customer in Rechnungen links to its partner page). A partner's page leads with its notes, prose the person writes and edits in place («Bearbeiten», «Speichern», «Rückgängig» in the toast; saved at once, with who changed them last from the history), then the rules the app carries out for it with how often they booked and «Ausschalten», then its bookings, receipts, payments and invoices of the open year, each a link, and, folded, how the app recognises it and what its files say. Wissen holds the pages of Arbeitsweise (one prose page per topic, «Neue Seite»), Lücken (who decided the open year's bookings, the payments that belong to no partner with «Regel anlegen», the open questions per partner) and Von der App (the public layer, read only). Notes live in `knowledge/<role>/<id>.md` beside the partner's file and `knowledge/company/<topic>.md`; `npm run check-processes` names notes of no partner and topics without a title or with a step that does not exist. The demo ships a few.
- A sentence of a partner's notes can become a rule the app carries out (docs/0.4.0/knowledge.md, issue #10 part 1). Pointing at a sentence offers «Als Regel vorschlagen»: a small form starts with the account, VAT code and private share the sentence names, «Vorschau» drafts the rule into the partner's file for the usual card, and «Die KI fragen» hands the sentence to the AI sidebar instead. Code, not a confidence score, decides whether it is offered: a rule for one partner or payer that no rule switched on already says, whose payments of the open year are booked today as it would book them. A sentence that leaves the choice open («meistens», «je nach Beleg») is offered only as an ask rule («Als Frage-Regel entwerfen»), one whose payments are booked otherwise stays a note, and the card says why; «Behalten» refuses what code does not offer. A kept rule quotes its sentence (`note: "…"`, `source: notes <date>`, which `npm run check-processes` checks), and the notes mark that sentence «führt die App aus», linked to the rule. When the person changes the sentence, nothing changes by itself: the rule asks «Notiz geändert: Regel anpassen?» with the sentence before and now, and «Regel anpassen» shows the rule as it is and as it would be, with «Trotzdem behalten» when this year's payments are booked otherwise. Switching the rule off or deleting it removes the mark, never the sentence. Firma › Regeln names such a rule «aus den Notizen». The booking AI does not read the notes yet (part 2).

In the list, each workflow has a status dot and its next scheduled start; workflows that cannot run yet move to «Einrichten» with how many steps are missing. - Two more kinds of Swiss firm keep their books here. ### Fixed
- **⚠ action** A card fee the bank debits as a payment of its own was booked twice (#24). ## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-09

### Added

- `books.json` and `belege.workspace.yaml` name their format version (`schema`). The app reads
  older files and migrates them in memory; it refuses a file from a newer version and says to
  update. `npm run update` writes the migrated files, with a backup in `data/backups/`.
- Seals of filed quarters: `npm run seal -- <YYYY-Qn> --close` closes a filed quarter and writes
  `filings/<year>/seal-<Qn>.json`, a hash of every entry, transaction and mapping up to its last
  day. Every load checks the seals. Books whose sealed part changed open read-only.
  `npm run doctor` reports the seals.
- `npm run update`: pulls the newest version, installs changed dependencies, migrates the
  workspace, then runs `doctor` and `test:books`. It stops at the first failure with the fix.
- `npm run init` sets up a company's workspace from 14 questions. `npm run doctor` checks the
  tools, the workspace and the mailboxes. A getting-started guide, a mailbox setup guide and a
  step-by-step quarter workflow.
- A second demo company, a one-person consultancy (`demo-consulting/`), with salaries and a
  correction (0535) paid.
- A casebook of 32 invented Swiss booking cases, checked against the VAT engine, which booking
  uses as examples when a company has little history of its own. `npm run mine-cases`.
- Bank statements (camt.053) of any Swiss bank. A statement lands on the bank account of its
  IBAN, else of its folder; nothing falls back silently.
- `npm run gaps`: the open transactions that no supplier, customer or authority file covers.
- Knowledge by role: the app ships supplier, customer and authority files (ESTV,
  Ausgleichskasse, cantonal tax office, BAZG); a workspace adds its own under `knowledge/`.
- Generic booking rules for payroll, social insurance, the VAT payment, the owner's account,
  direct taxes, refunds of sales and services abroad.

### Changed

- **⚠ action** `npm run seal -- <year>-Q4 --close` refuses: the fourth quarter closes with the
  year (`npm run year:close`), so the year-end entries can still be dated 31.12. `--force`
  closes it anyway.
- The app is called Vanilla Accounting. The workspace variable is `VANILLA_WORKSPACE`;
  `BELEGE_WORKSPACE` still works and prints a note.
- **⚠ action**: a workspace's `counterparties/` and `policy/` folders moved to
  `knowledge/{suppliers,customers,authorities,rules}/`. Run `npm run migrate-workspace` once and
  commit the workspace.
- One chart of accounts (Swiss KMU) for new workspaces. Booking works without a prior-year
  export.
- A manifest without `schema` reads as schema 1. Before, it was refused.

### Fixed

- An I-code on an investment account (1500 to 1799) is valid.
- An IMAP fetch closes the connection after a refused login.
- `doctor` treats a missing `beleg-import/` as a note: git does not keep the empty folder.

# Rechnungen (invoices)

Rechnungen (`/invoices…`, rebuilt in redesign phase 9) is the invoicing UI over `src/invoices/` (ADR 0010, ADR 0012) and its API (`/api/invoices`, `/api/customers`, `/invoices/<number>.pdf`, `/invoices/sent/<id>.pdf`). The sheet on screen is laid out by the PDF's own renderer (`src/invoices/sheet.ts`): blocks measured in the page, `paginate`, `assembleSheets`. Firma › Rechnungslayout (`/company/invoice-layout`) edits the manifest's `invoicing` block through `PATCH /api/workspace` and `POST /api/workspace/logo`. Records live in `$SB/invoices.json` and `$SB/transactional-data/<year>/invoices/`, the logo in `$SB/logos/`, not in the books.

## Sub-features

- `invoice-list`: `.row.inv[data-id=<number>|draft-<id>]` in groups Überfällig, Offen, Entwürfe, Bezahlt, Gutschriften, Storniert; filters `[data-status=open|overdue|draft|paid]` (`?status=`); the `Offen` total in CHF and the overdue notice; the views `[data-view=list|customers|sent]`. Issued invoices show in the period of their date and, while open, in later periods too (a link opens that period); drafts show in every period. Kunden and Versand have their own search.
- `invoice-detail`: head, facts (number, date, due, QR reference, VAT), status (paid, open, overdue with the last reminder, cancelled, credit note with its invoice, payment proposals with `Zahlung bestätigen`), actions `[data-act=send|remind|copy|credit|cancel|preview|issue|delete|remsend]`, `a.dl` (the issued PDF, D: `/invoices/<number>.pdf`; the send dialog and the Versand viewer use `/invoices/sent/<id>.pdf`), the sheet `.ivsheet`, margin sections (Rechnung or Gutschrift on a draft, Gestaltung on an issued invoice, `N. Mahnung` while writing one, Kunde, Gesendet, Kommentare, Verlauf). An open invoice links payments: `[data-testid=linked-payment]` with Lösen, `[data-testid=link-other-payment]` (select + Zuordnen), proposals with Zuordnen.
- `draft-edit`: on `.ivsheet.editing` every field carries `data-ed`: `date`, `dueDate`, `supplied` (Leistungsdatum or range), `intro`, `closing`, `customer` (select, `+` opens the customer form), `exemption` (with a 0 % line), `l:<k>:text|quantity|unit|price|vatRate` (`unit` and `vatRate` are selects); buttons `[data-ia=addline|rmline]` (`rmline` only with more than one line). A field saves when it is left (`PATCH /api/invoices/<id>`); a bad date or number is refused with a toast and the field shows its old value. The margin sets language and currency (CHF/EUR disabled without a pay-to account) and `eigene Texte · zurücksetzen`; a date before the last invoice warns.
- `issue`: `Ausstellen` gives the number, prints and stores the PDF; the Art. 26 gaps show above the actions; the client refuses first with `Zum Ausstellen fehlt: …`, the server's refusal reads `Für eine MWST-konforme Rechnung fehlt: …`.
- `send`: `Senden`/`Erneut senden` (`[data-act=send]`) always opens the send dialog `.isend`: without mail set up a hint and `[data-act=download]` (`PDF herunterladen`, recorded as sent to `download`), with mail `[data-act=mail]` (see «Sending by e-mail» below).
- `credit`, `cancel`, `reminder`: the dialogs `.idlg.icredit` (whole or part, reason, refund or offset when more than open) and the cancel dialog (reason, date, notice PDF; a paid invoice offers a credit note instead); `N. Mahnung` writes the reminder on the sheet (texts editable, fee in the margin), `N. Mahnung senden` (`[data-act=remsend]`) opens the same send dialog. The cancel dialog's `Storno-Mitteilung als PDF erstellen und als gesendet erfassen` is ticked by default.
- `customers`: `.row.cust[data-id]`, the detail with `[data-act=edit-customer]`, `[data-act=new-invoice]` (`/invoices/new?customer=<id>`), `[data-act=partner-page]` (Geschäftspartner), the customer's invoices; the form `.cdlg` per country (postcode name and shape, state, number first, VAT number kind, language from country and Swiss postcode).
- `sent`: `.row.sentrow[data-id]` by month; the detail shows the stored PDF (pdf.js, whole pages) with its SHA-256 and a link to its invoice.
- `invoice-layout`: font, colour swatches, logo upload/remove, logo place and size, envelope window, elements, the window frame (this browser only), and the default texts per language edited on a sample sheet (`{days}` stays a placeholder; `zurücksetzen` with undo); also `input[name=paymentTermsDays]`, `[data-testid=pay-to]`, `input[name=salutation]`, `input[name=mailSubject]`, `textarea[name=mailBody]`.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Dock `Rechnungen` (`G I`); `N` opens "Für welchen Kunden?" (in Kunden: the customer form); ⌘K finds invoices and customers.
- From a draft's margin `Schrift, Logo und Elemente: Firma › Rechnungslayout →`; from Abschluss' checks to the open invoices.

## Driving it with browse + sandbox.sh

- The demo's open quarter is 2026-Q2; its invoices 2026-001…006 are dated Q3, 2026-007 (cancelled) and the two drafts (`draft-in_8rkN6yKIRdjB` Muster Hotel, `draft-in_0gN7t9VlKZTs` Café Exemple) October: open `$UI/invoices?period=2026`. The next number is 2026-008.
- **Edit a draft.** Click a `.row[data-id^="draft-"]`, click `[data-ed="l:0:quantity"]`, `document.execCommand('selectAll')`, type, press Tab: `curl $API/api/invoices/<id> | jq '.lines[0].quantity'` shows the new value; the focus moves to the `l:0:unit` select. `[data-ia=addline]` puts the caret into the new line's text.
- **Issue and download.** On `draft-in_8rkN6yKIRdjB`, `[data-act=issue]`: toast `Rechnung 2026-008 ausgestellt`, the URL becomes `/invoices/2026-008`; `jq '.invoices[]|select(.number=="2026-008").pdf' $SB/invoices.json` gives `transactional-data/2026/invoices/2026-008.pdf` and a sha256 equal to `shasum -a 256 $SB/transactional-data/2026/invoices/2026-008.pdf`; `curl "$UI$(a.dl href)"` (`/invoices/2026-008.pdf`) serves the same bytes (driven 2026-10-31). Issuing renders the PDF in headless Chromium: see Gotchas.
- **Credit, cancel, reminder.** `[data-act=credit]`, `Teilbetrag`, an amount, `[data-act=credit-ok]`: a credit-note draft opens; `[data-act=issue]` issues it in the same sequence. `[data-act=cancel]` on an open invoice, a reason, `[data-act=cancel-ok]`: the stamp shows, Versand lists the notice. `[data-act=remind]` on 2026-003 (the one with reminder level 1, so the next is `2. Mahnung`; on 2026-10-31 «Überfällig» holds 2026-003, 005 and 006), a fee, `[data-act=remsend]`, then `[data-act=download]` in the dialog: Versand lists the reminder PDF.
- **Layout.** `/company/invoice-layout`, click a swatch, pick `Helvetica`: `$SB/belege.workspace.yaml` shows them under `invoicing.layout`; any draft shows the new colour and font.
- **Refusal.** Empty a draft's lines (`curl -X PATCH $API/api/invoices/<id> -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"lines":[]}'`; the UI keeps one line), `Ausstellen`: the toast says `Zum Ausstellen fehlt: mindestens eine Position`.
- `$S diff` stays `no changes` for all of this: invoices are not in the books (only a confirmed payment books an entry).
- **Sending by e-mail.** Firma › E-Mail-Versand (`/company/mail`) writes `outgoingMail` into the manifest; the password comes from `VANILLA_SMTP_PASSWORD` in the environment of `up` (`VANILLA_SMTP_PASSWORD=… $S up <run>`). A local SMTP server (smtp-server from node_modules, `allowInsecureAuth`, no STARTTLS) on 127.0.0.1 with security `none` captures the messages.
- Senden (`[data-act=send]`), the reminder's send (`[data-act=remsend]`) and Stornieren with the notice ticked open the dialog: `input[name=to|subject]`, `textarea[name=body]`, `[data-act=mail]` sends, `[data-act=download]` saves the PDF as before. Check the captured attachment's SHA-256 against the stored PDF and the sent log's `channel`, `to`, `messageId`.

## Gotchas

- Text typed into a contenteditable field saves only when the field is left (Tab, Enter, a click elsewhere); `browse type` alone does not save.
- Times of sent documents show in the browser's time zone; `sentAt` is UTC.
- The static audit export has no invoicing API: the area says so.
- PDFs (issue, send, reminder, notice) need the API's headless Chromium (`findBrowser` in src/invoices/pdf.ts, CHROME_PATH first). Known product bug (2026-10-31): an installed Google Chrome ends 12-18 s after the API starts it, and the API never starts it again, so every later PDF answers «Connection closed.». Start the sandbox with `CHROME_PATH="$HOME/Library/Caches/ms-playwright/chromium-<n>/chrome-mac-arm64/Google Chrome for Testing.app/Contents/MacOS/Google Chrome for Testing" $S up <run>` (sandbox.sh passes the caller's environment to the API).

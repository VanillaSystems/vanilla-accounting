# Geschäftspartner

Geschäftspartner (`/partners`, `/partners/<id>`) lists the suppliers, customers and authorities the books know, each with the owner's notes, the rules that book its payments, and what the open year's books hold for it. The notes are plain Markdown in `knowledge/<suppliers|customers|authorities>/<id>.md`; a sentence of them can become a rule the app executes, and the page marks the sentences a rule carries out.

## Sub-features

- `pt-list`: `.rows[role=listbox] .row.kwrow[data-partner="<id>"]` (selected: `.sel`, `aria-selected=true`), chips `.kwk` (`Notizen`, `Regeln n`, `Buchungen n`, `Rechnungen n`, `.kwk.app` = the app knows it), tabs `.counts button[role=tab]` (Alle, Lieferanten, Kunden, Behörden, Offen), search `label.search input` (`?q=`). With no id the first row is selected.
- `pt-detail`: `.detail-in.kw[data-for="<id>"]`, `h2.who`, open questions `.kwq` (links to the payment, receipt or entry), sections `section.kwnotes` (Notizen), `section.kwself` (Das macht die App selbst: acting rules `li[data-rule]` with `Ausschalten`/`Einschalten`, `Alle Regeln`, `Neue Regel für diesen Partner`), `.kwlinked .kwlink-g` (Buchungen, Belege, Zahlungen, Rechnungen for customers; at most 5 rows plus `und n weitere`), `details.kwdetails` (Details für die App: Erkennung, Belegarten, Konten, Sätze in der Datei, Von der App, Dateien). Customers link to `Kunde unter Rechnungen`.
- `pt-notes`: `button[data-act="edit-notes"]` (Bearbeiten) → `section.kwnotes.editing textarea` → `.kwnacts .btn.primary` (Speichern, also ⌘Enter) or `.btn.ghost` (Abbrechen, Esc). `POST /api/notes {kind:'save', ref:{kind:'partner',id}, text, basis}` writes `knowledge/<role>/<id>.md`; toast `Notizen gespeichert.` with `Rückgängig`; empty notes delete the file (`Notizen entfernt.`); a stale basis answers 409 `notesChanged`.
- `pt-propose`: `button.kwprop[data-act="propose-rule"]` (Als Regel vorschlagen) after each unmarked sentence `span.kwsent`, only for a partner with its own file. It opens `form.kwnoterule[data-testid=note-rule]` (Konto, `MWST-Code`, Privat %; prefilled by code from the sentence, no model). `Vorschau` → `POST /api/rules/note` drafts `data/draft/knowledge/<role>/<id>.yaml` (rule id `<id>-<account>`) and shows the rule card `div.ruledraft[data-draft]` with the payments it would hit. Code decides what it offers (`.noteoffer[data-offer]`): `rule` → `Behalten` (or `Trotzdem behalten`) and `Verwerfen`; `ask` (the sentence leaves a choice open, «manchmal») → `Als Frage-Regel entwerfen`; `note` (`Bleibt eine Notiz: n Zahlungen … sind heute anders gebucht.`) → only `Verwerfen` and `Ansehen`. `Behalten` → `POST /api/rules/draft {kind:'keep'}` writes `knowledge/<role>/<id>.yaml`; the card closes without a toast.
- `pt-mark`: `a.kwmark[data-mark="<ruleId>"]` («führt die App aus», title `Die App führt diesen Satz als Regel aus: …`) after a sentence that an acting rule carries as its `note`; a click scrolls to and flashes `li#rule-…`.
- `pt-changed`: `.kwself li[data-rule] .kwchanged` («Notiz geändert: Regel anpassen?», `Bisher: «…»`, `Jetzt: «…»` or `Der Satz steht nicht mehr in den Notizen.`), `button[data-act="adjust-rule"]` (Regel anpassen) drafts the changed rule (`replaces:`) on the same card.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Dock `Geschäftspartner` (`G P`), or ⌘K.
- From a customer in Rechnungen (`[data-act=partner-page]` on the customer; customers without a knowledge file have ids `cu_…`), from Wissen › Lücken (partner links of open questions), and from the setup conversation's partner card.

## Driving it with browse + sandbox.sh

Preconditions: a fresh demo sandbox. The demo's partners with own files are `gruenkaffee-muster` (yaml + md, 9 entries) and `swiss-post` (yaml + md, the one whose sentence code offers as a rule); `coop-pronto` has notes only (its rules are the app's, so no «Als Regel vorschlagen»), the customers come from `invoices.json`.

- **List and detail.** `$B goto "$UI/partners"`: the URL becomes `/partners/coop-pronto` (first row). `$B click '[data-partner="gruenkaffee-muster"]'`: the URL is `/partners/gruenkaffee-muster`, `.detail-in.kw` has `data-for=gruenkaffee-muster`, `.kwlinked h4` reads Buchungen 9 / Belege 9 / Zahlungen 9.
- **Notes edit (write).** `$B click '[data-act="edit-notes"]'`, put the caret at the end (`$B js "const t=document.querySelector('section.kwnotes.editing textarea'); t.focus(); t.setSelectionRange(t.value.length,t.value.length)"`), `$B type " Verify-Probe."`, `$B click '.kwnacts .btn.primary'`. Toast `Notizen gespeichert.`; the sentence shows in `.kwprose`. Proof: `diff demo/vanilla-accounting/knowledge/suppliers/gruenkaffee-muster.md "$SB/knowledge/suppliers/gruenkaffee-muster.md"` shows the one changed line, `git status --porcelain demo/` is empty, `$S diff` says `no changes` (notes are no part of the books).
- **Propose a rule (write).** `$B goto "$UI/partners/swiss-post"`, click the `[data-act=propose-rule]` inside the 4th `span.kwsent` («Wir buchen sie auf 6513 Porti und Versand mit 8.1 % MWST …»; `$B js "document.querySelectorAll('span.kwsent')[3].querySelector('[data-act=propose-rule]').click()"`): the form reads `6513`, `I81`. `Vorschau`: `.ruledraft` shows `Neu Post CH → Konto 6513 Porti und Versand, MWST I81`, `Hätte 2026 9 Zahlungen getroffen`, buttons `Behalten`, `Verwerfen`; `$SB/data/draft/knowledge/suppliers/swiss-post.yaml` exists. `Behalten`: the card closes (no toast), `$SB/knowledge/suppliers/swiss-post.yaml` gains `id: swiss-post-6513`, `then: { account: "6513", vat: I81 }`, `note:` and `source: notes <date>`; the sentence ends in `a.kwmark[data-mark=swiss-post-6513]` (title `Die App führt diesen Satz als Regel aus: …`), and `.kwself li[data-rule=swiss-post-6513]` lists the rule.
- **The other offers.** On `gruenkaffee-muster`: the Jutesäcke sentence (4210, «manchmal») gives `.noteoffer[data-offer=ask]`; the Rohkaffee sentence (4000) gives `Bleibt eine Notiz: 1 Zahlungen dieses Jahres sind heute anders gebucht.` (its Sammelzahlung of 30.09. is split on 4000 and 6000), so neither can be kept directly.
- **Changed note.** After «Propose a rule», edit that sentence (e.g. `6513 Porti und Versand` → `6570 Informatik`) and save: `.kwself li[data-rule=swiss-post-6513] .kwchanged` reads `Notiz geändert: Regel anpassen?`, `Bisher: «…»`, `Jetzt: «…»`. `Regel anpassen` opens a card titled `Notiz geändert` with a `Geändert` line (`vorher:` / `neu:`); `Verwerfen` leaves the rule as it was. An account the chart lacks (`6510`) keeps the old account in `neu:`.
- **Audit mode.** `READONLY=1 $S up ro`: no `Bearbeiten`, no `Als Regel vorschlagen`, no `Ausschalten`.

## Gotchas

- On fresh demo data neither the mark nor the changed prompt shows: the demo's partner rules have a plain string `then`, so none is an acting rule. Make one first (Propose a rule).
- Editing the textarea by `$B js` needs React's value setter: `Object.getOwnPropertyDescriptor(HTMLTextAreaElement.prototype,'value').set.call(t, v); t.dispatchEvent(new Event('input',{bubbles:true}))`; a plain `t.value = v` is not saved.
- `$B click` into the textarea puts the caret where it lands, and `$B type` inserts there. Set the selection with `$B js` first, or `fill` the whole text.
- A notes save commits to git when the workspace lies in a repository (subject `Notizen geändert: <id>`, the `Verlauf` link under the notes). The sandbox is no repository, so no commit is made and no `Verlauf` link shows. That is the sandbox, not a bug.
- `Die KI fragen` opens the AI sidebar and calls a model: in the sandbox it fails (claude shim).
- The headless browser's language is English unless set: `$B js "localStorage.setItem('language','de'); location.reload()"` (SKILL.md, Drive).

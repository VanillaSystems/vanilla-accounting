# Scenario: the first half hour of a one-person consultancy, with a score (clean image, no snapshot)

The third persona of [first-run.md](first-run.md): the same two runs, the same walk, the same 15
checkpoints and funnel weights, for a GmbH whose owner is its only employee. Read first-run.md
first; this file says only what differs.

## The persona

Matthias Kälin runs Kälin Advisory GmbH in Zug: IT and process consulting for small firms. He bills
Swiss clients at 8.1 % and one client in Berlin in EUR (paid into a Wise EUR account), pays himself
a salary, rents a desk in a co-working space, pays software subscriptions by card (some from the US
and Ireland without Swiss VAT), pays train tickets and client lunches with his private card and pays
himself back at each month's end, and uses the company phone privately too. Effective method, cash
basis, quarterly; a fiduciary did the first half year. Not an accountant; technical enough to be
impatient: he reads fast, clicks the first thing that looks right and expects the app to know what a
camt.053 is.

His words and his mistakes:

- He downloads the bank's PDF statement first (`ZugerKB_Kontoauszug_Q3_2026.pdf`), then the
  camt.053, and drops both.
- He drops the company credit card's statement (`Alpencard_Abrechnung_August_2026.pdf`) and expects
  its items booked; the bank shows only the card's monthly settlement.
- «Das isch privat zahlt, das wott ich zrugg.» He wants the receipts he paid privately reimbursed
  and does not know the owner's account (2451).
- «Telefon isch zur Hälfti privat.»
- He does not know the word Bezugsteuer and asks «wieso zahl ich MWST uf Software us Amerika?»

His files (`vm.sh up <run> --persona consulting`, generated by `persona-files.mjs`, module
`persona-files/consulting.mjs`): a Q3 2026 camt.053 of the Zuger Kantonalbank and one of a Wise EUR
account, a BkTxCd on every entry (customer payments, some naming the invoice, one without a number;
the June invoice paid in July; the salary; AHV and BVG; the co-working desk; the phone; software by
debit card in USD and CHF; the monthly reimbursement of his outlays; the credit card's settlement; the
Q2 VAT; the bank's fee; on Wise the payment from Berlin in EUR and two card subscriptions, one in USD
taken from EUR), his own six invoices of the quarter as PDFs (five Swiss at 8.1 %, one to Germany
without Swiss VAT, one still unpaid), supplier invoices (the co-working space and the phone with
8.1 % and a CHE number, a German tool registered for Swiss VAT, US and Irish tools without it, an
ad platform and a hotel at 3.8 % paid by credit card), the AHV and BVG bills, receipts he paid
privately (train tickets as PDFs, a restaurant, a book at 2.6 % and a parking ticket as photos), a
whiteboard photo that is no receipt, a duplicate, the bank's PDF statement and the card statement.

## The walk

As in first-run.md, with these steps where they differ:

2. New books for Kälin Advisory GmbH.
3. The setup: he takes «Consultant / services GmbH» when it is offered and checks the answers that
   matter here: employees **the owner only**, customers abroad **yes**, the owner pays business costs
   privately **yes**, private use **phone**, payouts **no**. He types one answer in his own words.
4. The first drop: everything in `~/Downloads` at once, the PDF statement and the card statement
   included. Watch: are the payments of the CHF account in once, not twice? Is the Wise account
   asked about as the firm's (it is) and then read? What does the app say about the card statement?
5. What the app asks: the sale question for a customer payment without its invoice (the June
   invoice paid on 03.07.: «Inland 8.1 %»; the payment from Berlin, if its invoice is not matched:
   «Ausland»), the receipts paid privately («Privat oder bar bezahlt»), the card settlement.
6. «The VAT so far» for Q3 and what is still missing.
7. `consult-ai` only: the wish «Telefon isch zur Hälfti privat» and the question «was muss ich
   jetzt no mache?».
8. The books behind the screen (`fetch('/books.json')`), against `answer-key.md`:
   - each customer payment 1020 / 3400 with V81, the one from Berlin on 1021 with V0-E at the ESTV
     rate of its day (Ziffer 221); the unpaid September invoice in no Q3 figure;
   - the US and Irish software B81-1 (Ziffer 383, deducted in 405), the German tool with its CHE
     number I81 and no Bezugsteuer;
   - no input tax on anything whose receipt shows no Swiss VAT, and none without a receipt;
   - every receipt paid privately on its expense account against 2451 with its rate (the book at
     2.6 %), each monthly reimbursement 2451 / 1020 without a code;
   - the salary 5000, AHV and BVG without VAT codes, the bank's fee row 6940;
   - the phone's private share: half on 2451 without VAT (`private: 50`) or whole with own use

## The score

The 15 checkpoints and the funnel of [first-run.md](first-run.md#the-score), unchanged.
«Payments booked by the app» counts the payments of both statements (the key names how many).

Three checkpoints only a consultancy of this kind has, in the last stage (weight × 1), after 15:

| # | Checkpoint | 2 means | Runs |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Customer payments | every customer payment booked with the code of the key: matched with its invoice where the invoice is among the files, else one sale question he answers at the first try; the payment from Berlin at the zero code in Ziffer 221; the bank's PDF statement brings no payment twice | both |
| C2 | Software from abroad | each service from abroad without Swiss VAT booked with Bezugsteuer (383 and 405, as the key); the German tool with Swiss VAT not; no input tax without a receipt that shows Swiss VAT; the card statement told as not imported, with what to do instead | both |
| C3 | Private outlays and the phone | each receipt paid privately over 2451 with its rate, each reimbursement settles 2451; the phone's private share follows his wish (or the setup's answer) and he can see how | both |

**1** for C1 to C3: he gets there with friction (a second try, a question he has to puzzle over, a
part booked by hand that the app could have booked). **0**: a payment on a wrong code, Bezugsteuer
missing or claimed on a receipt with Swiss VAT, input tax without its receipt, a payment counted
twice, a reimbursement booked as an expense, or he stops.

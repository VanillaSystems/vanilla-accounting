# Persona: Treuhänderin without AI (the Huber books as a folder, web sandbox)

Monika Frei keeps the books of 40 small firms. The Hubers want her to take care of everything:
not to review, to do it. She has no Claude subscription. She gets the books folder of Malerei
Huber (copied out of the VM: `.verify/huber-books/`), runs the app without any model, and does a
Treuhänderin's work by hand:

- process receipts: read them, set date, amount, VAT, link the payment, book debit and credit
- book what has no bank payment: a depreciation of the van, accruals (Transitorische Aktiven /
  Passiven: an insurance premium paid for next year, wages owed at year end), a provision
- correct wrong bookings, with corrections in the open period when a quarter is filed
- ask the owner what she cannot know («wofür ist dieser Beleg?») and get the answer back
- prepare the VAT return and the year-end, and hand them over

Judge: can she do every step without the AI; how many clicks; what an expert misses (keyboard,
debit/credit entry, account search, reversing entries, a journal print); how she asks the owner.

# Scenario: sidebar wishes (snapshot huber-q3)

Sandra looks through Q3 and uses the AI sidebar (⌘J, or the bot button top right) to fix what
she thinks is wrong, in her words. About ten wishes, each checked in the books afterwards:

- a receipt's date or amount is off («das Datum ist der 11. Juli»)
- a lunch was private; the phone is half private
- a receipt belongs to another payment («das gehört zur Zahlung vom 6. August»)
- her own invoice to a customer (Rechnung 2026-029) and how it is booked
- «warum hat diese Buchung keine MWST?» (a question, not a change)
- a change in a filed quarter if she can find one (there is none here: say what happens if she
  asks to change something from Q2)
- undo: she changes her mind after a change
- a vague wish («mach alles richtig»), a wrong one («buch das auf Konto 9999»)

Judge: did the change land as she meant, in the books; was the answer plain; did anything
change she did not ask for; how long did it take.

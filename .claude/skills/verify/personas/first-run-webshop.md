# Scenario: the first half hour of a webshop, with a score (clean image, no snapshot)

The second persona of [first-run.md](first-run.md): the same two runs, the same walk, the same 15
checkpoints and funnel weights, for a firm whose sales arrive as payouts of a payment provider. Read
first-run.md first; this file says only what differs.

## The persona

Corinne Wyss runs Teegarten Wyss GmbH in Sursee LU: a webshop on Shopify that sells Japanese tea
(2.6 %) and teaware (8.1 %), mostly to Swiss customers, a few to Germany, Austria, France and the
Netherlands. She imports teapots from Japan by DHL. Jana Imhof packs parcels two days a week; Corinne
pays herself a salary. Effective method, cash basis, quarterly; a fiduciary did the first half year.
Not technical, no accounting knowledge, Swiss German.

Her words and her mistakes:

- «Mini Uszahlige vo Shopify sind doch min Umsatz.» She thinks the payout is her turnover, and the
  first CSV she finds in Shopify is the payouts list (Finanzen › Auszahlungen › Exportieren): she
  drops `payouts_export.csv` instead of the orders export.
- She does not know the monthly «Shopify Payments Invoice» exists (Einstellungen › Rechnungen): in
  the first drop she leaves the three `Shopify_Payments_Invoice_*.pdf` out.
- «Durchlaufkonto? Weiss nöd, was heisst das.» She takes what the app proposes when it explains it.
- She calls the orders «Bestellige» and the payouts «Überweisige vo Shopify».

Her files (`vm.sh up <run> --persona webshop`, generated by `persona-files.mjs`, module
`persona-files/webshop.mjs`): a Q3 2026 camt.053 of the Luzerner Kantonalbank with a BkTxCd on
every entry (Shopify payouts several times a week, paid by Stripe with «SHOPIFY PAYOUT …» as the
reference; suppliers; a courier's customs bill; Shopify's monthly plan by direct debit; two orders
paid by bank transfer and the refund of one; wages; the AHV Akonto; the Q2 VAT; the bank's fee row),
Shopify's orders export and payments transactions export for Q3 in the columns of
`processes/suppliers/shopify.yaml`, the payouts export, Shopify's three monthly bills and three
Payments Invoices, a USD invoice from Japan without Swiss VAT, a Swiss invoice at 8.1 % and one at
2.6 %, two Post CH bills (domestic parcels 8.1 %, parcels abroad 0 %), DHL's customs bill and the
customs office's assessment (Veranlagungsverfügung MWST), a till photo of packaging material, a
product photo that is no receipt, and a duplicate PDF.

## The walk

As in first-run.md, with these steps where they differ:

2. New books for Teegarten Wyss GmbH.
3. The setup: she takes «Webshop / goods GmbH» when it is offered, then corrects what does not fit
   her: customers abroad **yes** (the preset says no), employees **staff**, imports **yes**, payouts
   from a webshop **yes**. One question she answers «weiss nöd».
4. The first drop: the statement, `payouts_export.csv` and every PDF and photo except the three
   Shopify Payments Invoices. Watch what the app says about `payouts_export.csv`: does it say it is
   not the report it needs, and which export to fetch in Shopify?
5. She fetches what the app asks for: drops `orders_export_1.csv` and
   `payment_transactions_export_1.csv`. The app asks once, in the drop row: the clearing account
   (it should propose 1093 «Durchlaufkonto Shopify»), the cut-over day (it should propose
   01.07.2026, the first day of the export), the revenue account and the fee account (6580, I0).
   She takes the proposals; on the cut-over day she first says «weiss nöd».
6. «The VAT so far»: Q3, honest about what is open: the mixed cart (tea and a teapot in one order,
   two rates) the app holds, the clearing account's balance and what it means. Then she finds the
   Payments Invoices and drops them: the VAT figure must change by the fee VAT the key names.
7. `shop-ai` only: the wish «Bestellige mit Tee und Channe zäme: teil das uf» on the held order,
   and the question «was muss ich jetzt no mache?».
8. The books behind the screen (`fetch('/books.json')`), against `answer-key.md`:
   - every Shopify payout 1020 / 1093 without a VAT code, none on a revenue account;
   - each order Shopify collected an entry of its own on 1093 / 3200 at the code its tax column
     shows: V81, V26, the one to Liechtenstein V81, the ones abroad V0 (Ziffer 220);
   - the fees 6580 / 1093 with I0, each charge on its day; each Payments Invoice's VAT booked once,
     without a payment;
   - the refund through Shopify on its own day with -V81, not on the order's day;
   - the two bank-transfer orders and the test order counted in the report's row and never booked
     onto 1093; the bank-transfer payments 1020 / 3200 V81 and the refund by bank -V81;
   - the courier's import VAT deducted from the assessment (– / 4030 M81-2, Ziffer 400), never from
     DHL's bill; DHL's own fee M81;
   - the goods from Japan without input tax; every other purchase at the rate its receipt shows.

## The score

The 15 checkpoints and the funnel of [first-run.md](first-run.md#the-score), unchanged. Checkpoint
12 counts the VAT figure after step 5 (orders and fees in), 13 counts the list of what is missing
then. «Payments booked by the app» counts the 62 payments of this statement.

Three checkpoints only a firm with a payment provider has, in the last stage (weight × 1), after 15:

| # | Checkpoint | 2 means | Runs |
|---|---|---|---|
| W1 | The provider's report in | the wrong file is named as not the report and the app says which Shopify export it needs; the orders export and the payments export are each taken as Shopify's; clearing account, cut-over day and accounts are asked once each, in her words, with a proposal she can take; nothing asked again in a later drop | both |
| W2 | Sales over the clearing account | every payout 1020 / 1093 without a code; every order Shopify collected booked on its own with the code of the key (counts of V81, V26 and V0 as in the key); the refund on its day; the bank-transfer and test orders counted, not booked; the held mixed cart says why in her words and offers a way to book it | both |
| W3 | Fees, the platform's VAT and import VAT | the fees' total as the key; each Payments Invoice's VAT booked once; the import VAT deducted from the assessment only; the clearing account's balance on 30.09. shown, and the difference to Shopify's balance (the June payouts) told as a fact she can act on | both |

**1** for W1 to W3: she gets there, but something is missing, needs a second try or a text she has
to puzzle over (a file only «nicht erkannt», a held order without a way on, a clearing balance below
zero without words). **0**: a payout or a payout report booked as turnover, an order on the wrong
code, a counted order booked, a fee or import VAT claimed twice or without its document, or she stops.

Made from the real Firma and Buchungen pages of the demo workspace (Muster Kaffee GmbH), captured read-only, with only the new parts added by hand in the app's own class names and colour tokens. The story: Anna Muster runs the café on Bexio and switches either in January 2026 with her 2025 journal, or on 1 July 2026 with Q1 and Q2 already filed from Bexio by her Treuhänder. She is happy to let the AI do the legwork and give it access.

The idea

  • Find out first, then ask. The app looks up the UID register (legal form, VAT registration, address) and reads the first file it gets (program, single or double entry, fiscal year). Only what changes the import plan is asked, one question at a time with answer buttons, in the AI column. What is known fills a list on the left with its source (UID-Register, aus deiner Datei, deine Antwort), and each row can be answered there by hand too.
  • The plan is written for this person. «Was wir von dir brauchen» lists exactly what these answers require, in this program's words. Nothing in the app is specific to Bexio: the AI writes the list, the export steps and the fetch code for the program it finds (ADR 0015).
  • «Wir holen es für dich» is the default. In order of preference: the program's interface with one access key (the AI writes a one-time source in the workspace), an export file (the AI explains it by the program's own menu names), the app's own browser window (the person logs in, the source reads), the Treuhänder (the AI drafts the e-mail, the person sends it). Each is the existing source card: its reach in plain words, «Prüfen» to see what it would fetch, then «Holen».
  • Review only where it matters. Fetching needs no review. The person looks closely at two things: a rule with the bookings that carry it, and a filed quarter before it is sealed.
  • A filed quarter is checked, then sealed. On a mid-year switch the first half year comes in as the full journal. Each filed quarter is recomputed from it and compared with the return as filed, Ziffer by Ziffer. A difference is explained with the booking behind it and resolved before sealing. After sealing the quarter never changes (golden rule 1), and a later correction goes through the 0535.
  • History is evidence, not knowledge. The 2025 journal is kept beside the books as examples. The AI and the classifier look things up in it and say so («wie 2025 gebucht»). Nothing in it binds the app on its own, and it never enters the books: they start on 1 January with the opening balances. On the mid-year path the first half year of 2026 does enter the books, and 2025 stays evidence.
  • A rule exists only once the person has seen its evidence and kept it. Where a partner was booked the same way every time (at least 4×), the app drafts a rule on the existing rule card, with the bookings that carry it. Its source reads «Buchungen 2025, bestätigt am …».
  • Three bins per partner on one screen: «Eindeutig» (a proposed rule, select all and keep), «Uneinheitlich» (no rule, one plain question), «Selten» (examples only, nothing to do).
  • Old mistakes are not learned. A pattern that breaks a rule of the app (input tax without a receipt that shows Swiss VAT, a foreign service without reverse charge, input tax on a private purchase) is shown as «so nicht übernehmen», with the reason in one sentence. The 2025 books stay untouched.
  • Few questions, the most money and the most payments first, one at a time, each skippable. A skipped question comes back at the next payment, not as a reminder.
  • Learning goes on while working. A rare partner is booked «wie 2025» with «Immer so?» under it. Firma › Wissen shows where each rule came from.
  • Wissen, not Regeln. What the app knows is more than booking rules: how it recognises a partner, where the receipt comes from, which document is valid proof, how payments line up with invoices, the cases the person decided, and what is still open. Firma › Regeln becomes Firma › Wissen, one page per business partner, with Regeln as one part of it and as a filter. Design: docs/0.4.0/knowledge.md.
  • The knowledge grows from the work. The app measures where it could not go on alone, per step and per partner, and shows each gap with the one answer or rule that closes it. Agreeing cases are offered as one rule, a note the app could carry out is offered as a rule, a rule that stopped matching or contradicts a newer decision is shown for review. Before the VAT return the AI asks only the open questions, all at once. Nothing new binds the app until the person keeps it.

0 · Gespräch

The switch starts as a conversation in the AI column, in Firma as a group «Umzug von Bexio» whose steps double as progress. The AI asks for the UID number and looks it up in the UID register: name, GmbH, address and the VAT registration fill the list on the left before anything is asked. Then it asks for any file from the old books. A Bexio journal export answers the program, double entry and the fiscal year, so two questions fall away. Without a file it asks the program (Bexio, Abacus, Banana, Excel, «Mein Treuhänder macht es») and single or double entry instead. Single entry means there is no journal to import: the app builds the year from bank statements and receipts. Then only the questions the register and the file cannot answer: the VAT method and period, per 1 January or in the running year, and who has the data. «Mein Treuhänder macht es» already answers the last one, so it is not asked again. The screen opens at the first question; the frame below is 0-gespraech.html#jan, played to the end.

Open questions

  • Where the switch lives. Here it is a group in Firma, opened by the conversation. It could also be the first screen of the desktop setup, which already asks whether the firm is new or switching (desktop.q.start). Firma keeps it reachable later, for instance when the Treuhänder sends the export a week afterwards.
  • The threshold for «Eindeutig». 4 identical bookings is a guess. Monthly partners pass easily, quarterly ones only just.
  • Where the examples live. The 2025 journal is outside the books, read only. It needs a place in the workspace (a folder of its own beside transactional-data/) and its own line in LAYOUT.
  • The UID register lookup. The public UID register has a web service (UID-Webservice of the BFS); whether the app may call it directly, and whether the VAT registration is in the same answer, needs checking. Method and period are not in it, which is why they are asked.
  • How far access goes. These owners will hand the AI an access key without hesitation. The mockup still shows the source card with «Prüfen» before «Holen» and switches the source off after one run. Is a one-time source the right shape, or should it stay as a live source until the year is closed (for receipts the Treuhänder adds later)?
  • Interface and portal facts. The Bexio interface (api.bexio.com, an access key the owner creates), its paths for accounts, journal, taxes and files, the ESTV ePortal address and whether it offers the filed returns as download are taken from memory and must be checked before anything is built. The app must not grow a Bexio branch: the AI writes these sources in the workspace.
  • Invoice date against payment date. Programs that book creditor invoices on the invoice date (Bexio with Kreditoren) put input tax into the wrong quarter for a cash-basis firm. The half-year check shows it per quarter, but the import should probably take the payment date from the start, and say so.
  • Changing an imported quarter before sealing. «Mit dem Zahlungsdatum übernehmen» changes the import of a quarter that is filed but not yet sealed, so that the books match the return. That reads golden rule 1 as «the books must say what was filed». Confirm this, and whether a difference whose cause stays unknown may be sealed at all.
  • Opening balances on a mid-year switch. With the full journal the books start on 1 January with the 31.12.2025 balance, and the switch date is only where the app starts booking. With account totals only, the opening is at the switch date, and the app's opening (one per year, config.openingBalances) would need a date other than 1 January, or the totals become one summary entry per account for the first half year. Which one?
  • Where the conversation lives. It is in the AI column of Firma. The desktop setup also asks new or switching first (desktop.q.start); the conversation could start there and continue here.
  • Bexio export details. The Bexio codes shown (UN81, VB81, …) and the export steps are placeholders until checked against a real Bexio export.
  • Partner file or folder. The design keeps one <id>.yaml per partner and adds an <id>.md for long procedures. A folder per partner would also hold a contract PDF. Which one?
  • Measuring the gaps. The automation figures need each run to record who decided an item (rule, case, note, AI from an example, person). The index records the origin of an entry today, not why the AI asked. Where that record lives is open.
  • When a pattern becomes a rule proposal. «3 cases decided alike» and «9 of 12 answers» are guesses for the threshold, like the 4 bookings of «Eindeutig».
  • Company facts. A new knowledge/company.yaml, or facts: in belege.workspace.yaml.

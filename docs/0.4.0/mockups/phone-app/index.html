Belege-App · Mockup

The iPhone receipt app (ios/Belege) redesigned with the Mac app's tokens and fonts and the brand «V». The phone on the left is clickable: scan, take pages by hand, finish, and watch a receipt get its name. Below it, every screen at rest. Demo data only. Notes for the Swift work are in NOTES.md.

Try it

  • Scannen: the camera outlines the receipt, but nothing is taken until you tap the shutter. Turning pages captures nothing. Take a few pages, tap a page in the strip to retake, move or delete it, then Fertig.
  • The new receipt appears at the top with a name the phone read itself (marked vorläufig). A moment later the Mac has read it, and the name and amount are confirmed.
  • Tap a receipt for its detail. Umbenennen lets you set the name by hand.
  • Tap the connection badge to cycle its states: connected, the Mac's «Handy» switch off, Mac not found, not paired (opens pairing).
How a receipt gets its name

(a) On the phone, right after the scan. Apple's text recognition (Vision) reads the first lines and a total. Fast, offline, and a guess: shown as vorläufig.

(b) From the Mac, once it has read it. The Mac's reading is the real one (vendor, total, VAT, matched payment). Today the phone can't ask for it: the phone listener only has /scan, /api/status and /api/upload. It needs one new read-only route, for example GET /api/uploads?names=…, with the same token.

Recommendation: both. (a) at once, so the list never shows a bare timestamp; (b) replaces it when the Mac has read the receipt. A name you type yourself wins over both.

Why a custom camera

Today's scanner is Apple's VNDocumentCameraViewController. Its public API has no setting for auto-capture: the Auto/Manual switch lives inside Apple's screen and starts on Auto. So it takes pictures on its own while pages are being turned. A camera of our own (AVFoundation with Vision's rectangle detection) outlines the receipt live and captures only on the shutter. An «Auto» switch is there, off by default.

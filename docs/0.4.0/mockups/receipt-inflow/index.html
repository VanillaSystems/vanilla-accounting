The idea in three sentences

  • What's measured is payments, not vendors. The question that matters is «does every payment have its receipt?». That also covers a one-off purchase from a shop nobody has bought from before: a connected mailbox catches it anyway.
  • Ways in are set up per mailbox or folder, not per vendor. Only a vendor whose receipts never come by mail (Shopify's bill, which is only in the admin) needs a way of its own.
  • Bank statements stay manual: camt.053 or PDF from e-banking, dragged in whenever it suits you, with no reminder. The app only shows how far the payments go («Zahlungen bis 31.08.»). Not through a browser session into the bank.

2 · «Einrichten»: the AI sets it up with you

«Einrichten» opens the existing AI sidebar with a setup conversation. First the app checks what it can on its own: payments without a receipt, which provider hosts the domain's mail (an MX lookup), and which accounts Apple Mail has. Under «Details» you can see what it looked up. Then it asks only what it can't know («Kommen dort auch private Mails an?»), and proposes a plan as a card. Nothing runs until you take it. A mailbox password goes into the app's form, never into the chat.

What the setup AI is told

Its mission comes first. The rules come from a new step in the existing guide.

# processes/receipt-collection.guide.yaml · new step (sketch)
setup:
  goal: >-
    Automate as much of the receipt collection as possible, for the benefit of the
    person using the app: every payment should find its receipt without them doing
    anything. Ask only what only they know, and use the least access that does the job.
  rules:
    - id: setup-local-client
      when: Apple Mail or Outlook classic already has the account
      then: propose reading it there; no new login needed
    - id: setup-bank-manual
      when: bank statements
      then: the person drags in the export from e-banking (camt.053 or PDF) when it suits them; show how far the payments go, never remind; never log into a bank
    - id: setup-no-secrets
      when: always
      then: passwords go into the app's form, never through the chat
  precedents: []

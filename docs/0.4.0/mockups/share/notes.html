How it was made: the app ran read-only on the demo workspace (Muster Kaffee GmbH, READONLY=1, a copy outside the repository); its rendered DOM and CSS were captured with puppeteer-core (.verify/share-capture/capture.mjs, not in git: it is the same recipe as earlier mockups). app.css is the app's CSS as rendered; share.css and share.js add only the new parts with the app's tokens and class names. The website page and the e-mail use the company website's styles (site/site.css, a subset of its site.css) and its logo's red mark. The people are made up: Anna Muster (the demo firm's owner) and her father Peter Muster, a fiduciary, whom she calls «Papa».

The figures are the demo's: Q3 2026 zu bezahlen 2'146.30 today; Papa's changes move it to 2'151.33 (the 30 % private share takes 5.03 of input tax); the Q1 correction shifts 85.56 from Ziffer 400 to 405 and leaves the amount due unchanged; 1510 grows by 806.88 + 1'141.89.

Decisions to look at

  1. The company's name, domain and logo are stand-ins. check-public allows the company's name, its domain and its GitHub org only in README.md (IDENTITY_FILES in src/publish/leak-guard.ts, your decision of 2026-10-09), so the website and the e-mail say «Vanilla Accounting» and vanilla-accounting.ch, the logo is its red mark with a wordmark, and the release links point nowhere. Everything else is the website's look. To show the real ones, the mockup folder would have to join IDENTITY_FILES.
  2. Where «Teilen» lives: a chip in the context bar next to Export (every area, ⇧S), whose status card replaces a separate screen. The spec (PR #514, B10) puts «Bücher übergeben …» in Firma › Personen and Änderungen.
  3. No hold while shared. The flow lets Anna keep booking; the spec recommends holding her writes (Q-B8) so that the return is a fast-forward. Without the hold, a record both changed is a conflict, hence the «Deine Version | Papas Version» row. That needs a record-by-record merge (ADR 0003 §3), which the spec calls much larger than the baton.
  4. Approve per group, as decided with you, with «Alles übernehmen» on top. The spec's Q11 recommends the fast-forward as the take-over and undo per row afterwards. In the prototype every row stays undoable after «Übernehmen».
  5. A hosted, end-to-end encrypted relay instead of the spec's .vanilla file: the package lies on the company's server, the key is in the link after the # (never sent to the server), the link expires after 10 days and the package is deleted then. This needs new infrastructure: storage, sending the e-mail, the return channel, and the website page. No PIN means whoever has the e-mail can open the books. «Zurückziehen» cannot take back what he already opened; the card says so.
  6. «Prüfmodus» is taken. The read-only mode already shows «Prüfmodus · nur Lesen». The recipient's mode is labelled «Prüfmodus für Treuhänder» as you asked; one of the two should get another name.
  7. The recipient files nothing: «Abrechnung erstellen» is replaced by «Einreichen macht Anna» (the fiduciary role of the spec, B2). A correction of a filed quarter is a correction booking with corrects (golden rule), dated in the first open quarter (Q2, since Q2 is not filed in the demo).
  8. Apple chip or Intel: only Chromium browsers report the architecture (navigator.userAgentData); Safari does not. The page picks the Apple-chip dmg and offers Intel as a small link. The dmg comes straight from the GitHub release (Vanilla.Accounting-0.5.0-arm64.dmg, 129 MB).
  9. Windows and phone: «gibt es zurzeit für den Mac», with the books read-only in the browser. That view is the app's UI decrypting the package in the browser, like the audit export: a new build target.
  10. Size: the first share carries 2026 (64 MB, closed years stay with Anna, as in B3.2); later ones only what is new (12 MB); his return only his changes (48 KB).
  11. Where the mockup lives: docs/0.4.0/mockups/share/, beside the earlier mockups, because docs/0.4.0/** is already in public-files.txt. docs/mockups/ would need a new line there.

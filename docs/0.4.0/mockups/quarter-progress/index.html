How far along is the period?

Revised after the owner's review: variant 5 now counts done / total at every station, splits the open part into «macht die App» and «braucht dich», merges Beleg and Zuordnung into Belege, compares with the periods before and lists the recurring payments that have not come. Five ways the app could show the open reporting period's way to a fileable VAT return instead of the activity summary («18 Belege gesammelt, 18 gebucht»). Each frame is the real Automatisierung page of the demo (captured from a read-only server); only the summary is swapped, plus the period chip in the top bar that the page follows. Every number comes from one pass over the payments of the selected period: not cumulative, a payment counts at the first station it has not passed. Blue means the app is still on it, amber means it waits for you, green is done. Links in the frames show where they would go.

Which numbers are real

Real means read from the demo workspaces' books (demo/: Muster Kaffee GmbH, quarterly, effective method; demo-schreinerei/: Schreinerei Beispiel GmbH, half-yearly, net tax rate 4.5 %), or from npm run vat-report on them. The due date is the app's own rule (period end + 60 days). Every count in variant 5 is computed in the frame from the payment list, never typed in: the same pass that counts Q3 counts Q2 and Q1 for the comparison, and the rhythm of «Erwartet, noch nicht da» comes from the three months before the period.

Dock cards: each area's state for the open period

After the owner's review (#33): every dock icon's tooltip is a card in the route's station style, for the open reporting period, whatever the picker shows (so «Picker: filed Q1» shows the same cards as «Before deadline»). Belege, Buchungen, Kontoauszüge with Kontrolle, and MWST (station Abrechnung) render the very same station markup as the route above (one function, stationHtml, two places), fed by the same pass for the open period; the other areas get a card of the same shape. The cards are light popovers (the page surface with a line and shadow) in both themes, not the dark tooltip: the station's colours (amber «braucht dich», blue «macht die App», green done, the soft pill backgrounds) are tuned for the page surface, and an inverted tooltip would need a second palette for every piece. Kontrolle sits on Kontoauszüge, under the statements station: the area is «Auszüge pro Konto, Saldo geprüft», and the check compares the statement's balance with the books. Holding G (or «All») hides the cards beside the icons, keeps the dock's key caps and shows all twelve cards as a board of six columns beside the dock: one card per icon in dock order, each with its G key, so nothing overlaps; the cards beside the icons would cover each other at the dock's 48 px pitch. In the full-size frame, real hover and holding G both work.

Marks on the icons: no numbers. An area where something waits for you (a receipt missing, a question, a statement to upload) gets a quiet 6 px dot (amber at 65 %, no outline); together they are the «braucht dich» that Automatisierung's card counts. No number anywhere on the bar (the owner dropped the period ring at the foot of the bar): Automatisierung stays the app's disc at the top, its state only in its card. The dot sits top-right beside the glyph, outside its 24 px box and level with its top, a status dot beside the icon rather than on it; «At the bar's edge» puts it at the row's right edge by the bar's border for comparison (checked on the receipt, bank and book icons, light and dark, 100 % and 125 %). An area where only the app works gets nothing: the app's work asks nothing of the person, and any mark invites a click that leads nowhere. Overdue invoices (Rechnungen) and an expected payment that has not come (Geschäftspartner) are facts about others, not tasks, so they show in their cards and never as a dot; Bilanz & ER, Änderungen and Firma never get a mark. «Nichts» removes the dots too: then only the cards say what waits.

Recommendation

Take 5: the route on top, the checklist under it, the dock cards beside it. The route answers «where does it stick?» and «is that mine or the app's?» at a glance, in the order the work really flows, and each station opens its items. The checklist answers the question that drives the work, «can I file?», with the deadline as a plain date and one button per open condition. They need each other: the route alone does not say when you are allowed to file, the checklist alone hides where the flow stands.

The dock cards keep the open period in reach while you browse a filed one, without nagging: a quiet dot where something waits, the counts only on hover or while G is held. The timeline (3) is the most honest picture of time («complete through 11.08.») and could replace the statements row of the checklist later, but it needs reading and gets crowded with more banks. The dots (4) are pretty and scale badly: a firm with 300 payments a quarter gets a carpet, and the colour carries all the meaning.

How variant 5 counts, after the review. The bank statement is the backbone: per payment, is its receipt there, is it booked (cash basis). So the person's key figure is payments without a receipt; a receipt that came but is not matched is the app's work and only a grey sub-line. A payment that waits for its receipt is counted once as «fehlt → dich» under Belege and, under Buchungen, as the app's («davon 1 wartet auf den Beleg»): the app books it as soon as the receipt is there, so the person is not asked twice. Kontrolle compares the last month-end balance with the books; a difference that equals the unbooked payments is the app's and resolves itself. «Erwartet, noch nicht da» does not block filing: on cash basis a payment that did not happen declares nothing. It is a hint that a statement, a direct debit or a bill may be missing, and links to the partner.
